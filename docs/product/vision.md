# Product Vision

**Status**: Confirmed objective; scope and acceptance are pending owner review.

Documentio aims to help an enterprise turn its document corpus into traceable, searchable knowledge and let authorized users ask questions whose answers are grounded in source evidence.

The intended value chain is: source documents are accepted under policy, extracted with provenance, transformed into retrieval units, indexed for lexical and vector search, and used as bounded evidence for conversational answers with source references. Operators need visibility into ingestion, synchronization, failure, and deletion outcomes.

This is a product objective, not a claim that the system exists or that any model/service has been selected. User populations, approved corpus, client experience, data governance, SLOs, and budget are unresolved. See [scope](scope.md) and [open questions](../project-management/open-questions.md).
