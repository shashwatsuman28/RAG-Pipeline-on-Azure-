# Data Protection

**Status**: Policy pending. Treat content as potentially sensitive until the data owner classifies it.

Protection scope includes original files, extraction output, tables, chunks, embeddings, metadata, model prompts/answers, citations, conversation state, caches, logs, backups, and change records. The project owner must decide classification, encryption/key management, allowed regions, service data-processing conditions, retention, legal holds, deletion, backup, redaction, and incident notification obligations.

Do not use real enterprise content in portfolio demonstrations without explicit written authorization and a documented handling policy. Do not claim regulatory compliance, residency, customer-managed key use, or full deletion until the exact service configuration and residual copies have been reviewed and tested.
